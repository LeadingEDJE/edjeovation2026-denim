The iOS flow starts with the shopping moment: what the customer is trying to solve, which colors they want to focus on, and what they want to avoid.

Style signals turn those preferences into a Muse profile, then the customer chooses the catalog scope for the appointment.

The upload step is where the integration becomes more powerful. The customer can add a photo of an outfit or piece they already own. In this demo, the image is read as a clean white tank, olive utility pant, and blue plaid layer. The app converts that photo into structured styling context: visible garments, colors, material cues, style keywords, and an actionable pairing note.

The photo itself is not stored. Only the text analysis moves forward into the appointment, where it can guide product suggestions and associate prep.

After the customer confirms the outfit context, they choose the store, select an available time, add final guidance, and book the appointment with the assigned stylist.
