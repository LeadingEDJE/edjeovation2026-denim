The associate opens the appointment from the active queue and gets the customer story in one place: timing, store, stylist, occasion, fit profile, order-history signal, color direction, style signals, and the outfit analysis from the uploaded photo.

Recommendations are designed as associate support, not autopilot. Deterministic scoring first narrows the catalog around fit, size, color, and constraints. Claude can then re-rank the shortlist and write concise rationales when available. If AI is unavailable, the workflow still works from the rule-based order.

From the product prep view, the associate can mark items as pulled, add prep notes, message the customer, check the customer in, capture session notes, write a customer-facing recap, and complete the appointment without switching tools.
