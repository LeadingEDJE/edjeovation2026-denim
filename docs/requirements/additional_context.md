The submission form for the user should collect some information as well as the date and time of their appointment
The goal should be for the store associate to have a solid understanding of what the user would like to accomplish in the fitting.
Asking probing questions like: is there an upcoming event you are looking to shop for, are there specific colors you would like to focus on or avoid, can you describe your style, etc.
do not want to burn the user out on too many details, but want to give the associate a good understanding of what the user is looking for.
Suggestion I made was around linking these to the "Abercrombie Muses"
1. Clean Muse
Style: Minimal, pared-back, polished
Traits: Effortless, not fussy, elevated simplicity
Role in assortment: Core essentials + timeless styling anchor

2. Romantic Muse
Style: Feminine, soft, effortless
Traits: Ease, flow, subtly dressed-up
Role: Brings femininity and emotional/aspirational appeal

 3. Boyish Muse
Style: Preppy, menswear-inspired, sporty
Traits: Relaxed, casual, slightly androgynous edge
Role: Broadens appeal into casual + sporty outfitting

4. Statement Maker
Style: Trend-forward, bold, fashion-leading
Traits: Expressive, pushes boundaries
Role: Drives fashion credibility + newness in assortment
Described as “pushes the envelope… most trend forward”




The user's order history should also be available to help guide the stylist
this should be combined with the user input

The stylist should be ready for the appointment
they should have pulled some styles in anticipation for client to arrive, these should be curated items based on the user input and client order history
once the appointment starts, they would be on their own to work with the client to fully understand what they would like

The system should have a way to assign stylists near-submission time to a client.
the client should get a confirmation for their appointment and who their stylist is.

Store associates should have a way to see upcoming appointments and who the assigned stylist is
the likely should have the ability to re-assign stylists in the case where the person is not a good fit, or isn't available, etc.
some store associates are very qualified to do this styling, for some people, this is just a job, but we want to target specific stores with qualified associates
just assume the "store" we are emulating has the ability to make it work

Users should have a mechanism to directly communicate with their stylist
this would likely need to be something async since the stylist is also a store associate and would not be available to answer at all times
a shared inbox, or some kind of messaging system that all stylists can see might be a good candidate for this
Additionally, some way to simply add more information to their appointment to give the stylist more guidance

The initial MVP should be focused on non-peak hours
The intent here is to give the person an amazing, curated experience, without the associate being pulled away for other customers
non-peak hours: Monday-Thursday : Anytime

Another scope item: A&F Associate is able to trigger a "Stylist Appointment Recap" after the customer leaves the store (regardless if they purchased something during their visit or not)
