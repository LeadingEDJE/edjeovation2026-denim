ai_usage_explanation.md — AI Usage Explanation

This is one of the most important documents in your package. It explains how and why AI is part of your solution. Judges are specifically evaluating whether AI is used appropriately, deeply, and responsibly — not just mentioned.

Required sections:


* How AI Is Used — Walk through every place AI appears in your solution
* What model or tool is used?
* What does it do at that step?
* What pattern is it following (e.g., RAG, classification, generation, summarization)?
* Why AI Is the Right Fit — Explain why AI is the appropriate technology for this problem
* What would the solution look like without AI?
* Why does AI solve it better?
* Level of AI Dependency — Classify your AI usage:
* Assistive — AI enhances the experience but the core product works without it
* Integrated — AI is a significant component but not the only one
* Core (AI-Dependent) — The product cannot function without AI
* Responsible AI Considerations — Address at least 3 of the following:
* How do you handle hallucinations or incorrect outputs?
* How is user data protected?
* Is the system transparent about being AI-powered?
* Are there human oversight or escalation mechanisms?
* Are there any bias or fairness considerations?
* How do you handle edge cases or out-of-scope inputs?
