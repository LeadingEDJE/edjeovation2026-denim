project_summary.md — Project Summary

This is your project's front door. It should give any reader — technical or non-technical — a clear understanding of what you built, why it matters, and who it's for.

Required sections:


* Project Name — The name of your project
* Team Members — Full names and roles (e.g., Tech Lead, Designer, etc.)
* Client — The client or business unit this is built for, if applicable
* Problem Statement — Describe the problem you are solving (max 200 words)
* What is broken, missing, or painful today?
* Who is affected and how?
* Why does this problem matter?
* Proposed Solution — Describe what you built (max 300 words)
* What does it do?
* How does it work at a high level?
* What makes it different from doing nothing or using an existing tool?
* Target Users — Who uses this? List primary, secondary, and tertiary users if applicable
* Key Benefits — 3–5 bullet points summarizing the value delivered
