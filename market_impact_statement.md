market_impact_statement.md — Market & Impact Statement

This document makes the business case for your project. It should convince a skeptical stakeholder that this solves a real problem, for real people, with real economic value.

Required sections:


* Target Customer / Client Persona — Describe the primary user or buyer in detail
* Their role, company size, and context
* Their specific pain points
* What they are looking for in a solution
* Business Value / ROI — Quantify the value where possible
* Time saved, cost reduced, revenue enabled, risk mitigated
* Even rough estimates with stated assumptions are better than nothing
* Competitive Alternatives — What exists today that someone might use instead?
* Why is your solution better, different, or more appropriate?
* Why This Matters Right Now — Make the case for timing
* What market, technology, or business conditions make this the right moment?
* Why would a client or stakeholder care about this today?
